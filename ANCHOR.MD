# Anchor

Invariants for *Little Jam Band Tour*. Change rarely; if you do, log why in `DECISIONS.MD`.

## Pitch

Old-school rogue-lite text adventure (Oregon Trail / Little Miami Vice energy). You play tour kids following a jam band across the country — hustle money, tickets, beer and other substances, rides, and luck. Survive the road. Make the show. Have the best time ever.

## Player fantasy

- You are a crew of 1–5 tour kids, not the band.
- The fantasy is the lot, the shakedown, the ride, the scramble for tickets — getting in and being there.
- Wins feel like: made the show stocked, crew intact, maybe nailed a setlist bet.
- Losses feel like: missed the show, jailed, twisted ankle, died of wook flu, stranded with an empty tank.

## Core fantasy loop (always true)

Arrive near a venue → hustle on shakedown and nearby spots before showtime → get (or sneak) the crew in → experience the show → post-show recap → prep the next travel leg.

Travel between cities is resource pressure and narrative risk. The destination hub is always shakedown-shaped.

## Tone

Comedic, grounded road-culture text adventure. Multiple-choice narratives with sharp outcomes. Substances and sketchy choices exist as systems (resources, risk); keep it gamey, not instructional.

## Non-goals (for now)

- Not a rhythm game or live music simulator.
- Not a full 3D world or open map — screens, text, dropdowns, light art panels.
- Not a competitive multiplayer service.
- Not a realistic crime sim — outcomes are story/systems flavor.
